# Documentation guides

- Create new guides in `docs/guides_to_be_validated/`.
- Keep these drafts local and ignored by Git. Do not force-add them, link to them
  from public documentation, or include them in generated documentation or
  distribution packages.
- A guide may become public only after the user approves it for publication.
  After approval, move it to its public location, add it to Git, and update the
  relevant links.
- Maintain existing approved guides in their current public locations.
